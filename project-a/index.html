[ 3rd Brother ]

Scientific Name:

Solis Ostium

Discovered by / at:

Astronomer Maxwell Cowan, Discovered circa 2026

Family:

Emerging Asteroid / Planetary with basic Sentience.

Habitat:

From the Stratosphere, though its instability requires further research.

Appearance:

A ball of pure fire and gas. Contains mountain ranges which from a far look like teeth. Its core is visible which is extremely rare, and can be blind you if you squint at it for too long. Proceed with caution!

Key Characteristics:

  • bright red with fangs
  • darts around
  • extremely small
  • follows no orbit unlike its neighbors

What we know so far about 3rd Brother? It is said to be born from volcanic rock that was launched into space, although there is no explanation for why it remains caught between diﬀerent gravitational planes. As you can see, it does not have an orbit or path that it follows. It is a lone wolf and is highly misunderstood. It does not trust anyone from its species easily, and although you are different...3rd Brother has taken an interest in you despite its hostility. It appears to be able to communicate with humans telepathically which is highly usual for its species. Because of this distinct biological feature, it necessitates the creation of an entirely new subspecies called "Solis Ostium" after its mouth like hole in its center.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature This creature is highly misunderstood, meaning that it is subject to spurts of trust and distrust, never letting one get too close while also trying to communicate its needs to you. Please do not make direct eye contact and proceed with caution in engaging with this psychological outcast.
